Eight-row vector process diagram showing legal CMER transitions G01 through G08. Proposed to underway (G01), underway to results deposited (G02), results deposited to screened (G03), screened to independently checked (G04) or not independently verifiable (G05), independently checked to synthesis eligible (G06), any non-withdrawn state to withdrawn (G07), and not independently verifiable back to screened after a revised packet (G08). Each row specifies its decision guard. Roles are contributor, dual screeners, independent verifier and adjudicator.
